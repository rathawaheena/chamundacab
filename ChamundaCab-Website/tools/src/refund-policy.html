Refund & Cancellation Policy

Plans change. This page explains how much you get back when you cancel a booking with Chamunda Cab Tourism, how long refunds take, and what we do if your driver is late or the cab breaks down.

  • Refund in 5–7 business days
  • Support 24/7

1.Cancellation & Refunds

2.How to Cancel

  1. Contact usCall +91 81282 95920 or WhatsApp +91 63521 79909, or email us.
  2. Share booking detailsGive your name, phone number, pickup date and time, and route so we can find your booking.
  3. Get confirmationWe confirm the cancellation time and the refund amount to you in writing on WhatsApp or email.

The cancellation time is the time we receive your request by call, WhatsApp or email, not the time you decide to cancel. Please inform us as early as possible.

3.Refund Timeline

Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days, back to the original mode of payment.

  • Business days are Monday to Saturday, excluding bank and public holidays.
  • The time is counted from the day we confirm your cancellation and refund amount.
  • UPI or bank transfer payments are returned to the same UPI ID or bank account that paid. Cash advances, if any, are returned to the bank account or UPI ID that you share with us.
  • After we process the refund, your bank or payment provider may take a little extra time to show it in your account.
  • If you have not received the refund after 7 business days, please contact us with your booking details and we will check it.

4.Driver Delay / Cab Breakdown

If we fail to give you the service you booked, you should not have to pay for it.

  • Cab breakdown or driver unavailable. If your cab breaks down, or the assigned driver cannot reach you, we will arrange an alternate cab of the same category or better as quickly as we can. If we cannot do that, or you prefer not to continue, you get a full refund of the amount paid for the unused portion of the trip.
  • Driver delay at pickup. If our driver is late and you choose to cancel because of it, you get a full refund. If you wait and take the ride, please tell us your concern so we can look into it.
  • Breakdown during a trip. We will send a replacement vehicle to take you on. The fare is calculated only for the distance actually covered with the original vehicle, and you pay nothing for the part we could not complete.
  • Cancellation by Chamunda Cab. If we cancel a confirmed booking for any reason, you get a full refund or a new booking at your choice.
Delays caused by traffic, accidents, road closures, weather or other events outside our control are not treated as driver delay, as explained in our Terms & Conditions.

5.Important Notes

  • Rescheduling. Moving your trip to another date or time is treated as a cancellation if less than 12 hours remain. Please ask us, and we will try to help.
  • Fare and extras. Refund percentages apply to the amount you paid in advance. Toll, parking and taxes paid on your behalf during a completed trip are not refundable.
  • Customer-requested changes. If you cut a trip short after it has started, the fare for the confirmed trip remains payable, as agreed at booking.
  • Fraud awareness. We will never ask for your card PIN, OTP or banking password to give a refund.
  • Updates. We may update this policy. The version on this page when you book applies to your booking.

Booking & Rates

How to book, fares and what is included.
How do I book a cab with Chamunda Cab?

You can book 24/7 in three easy ways:

  • Call +91 81282 95920.
  • WhatsApp +91 63521 79909.
  • Use the Cab Booking page on our website.

Share your pickup and drop location, date and time, number of passengers and preferred car. Your booking is confirmed once we confirm it to you on call or WhatsApp, along with the fare and driver details.

What is the difference between a one-way and a round-trip booking?

One-way: you travel from your pickup to a single drop location, and the fare covers that journey. It suits airport transfers, city-to-city rides and drops.

Round-trip: the cab stays with you for the whole journey, including the return, so the fare is based on the total distance and the days of the trip. It suits sightseeing tours, pilgrimages and family trips.

We confirm the exact fare for either type before the trip. Check our All Routes page for popular routes.

What are your per-km rates?

Indicative rates are WagonR hatchback from ₹11/km, Sedan (Swift Dzire) ₹12/km, Ertiga ₹14/km, Innova ₹17/km and Innova Crysta ₹20/km. Tempo Travellers are available for larger groups.

The final fare depends on the route, vehicle and season, and is confirmed before you travel. See Our Cars for details.

Is the fare fixed? Are there hidden charges?

We confirm a fixed fare before the trip, and we do not add surge pricing. Extra charges apply only in the cases explained at booking: tolls, parking and state taxes, night charges, and extra distance, hours or waiting beyond what was agreed.

Are toll tax and state tax included in the fare?

Unless we tell you otherwise at booking, toll, parking and state entry taxes or permits are extra and are paid as per actual receipts. We explain what applies to your route when we give you the fare, so there are no surprises on the road.

Do you offer local city packages?

Yes. Local packages such as 8 hours / 80 km are available within city limits. Extra hours and kilometres beyond the package are charged at the confirmed rate, and outstation rates apply for trips outside the city.

Do you provide airport pickups and drops?

Yes, we serve airports across India, including Rajkot (Hirasar), Ahmedabad, Delhi, Mumbai and more. Share your flight number when booking so we can track arrival and avoid delays.

Cab Services & Safety

Our drivers, cars and night travel.
Are your drivers verified and experienced?

Yes. Our chauffeurs are experienced on city and highway routes, are polite, and speak Hindi, Gujarati and English. Your driver’s name and contact number, and the vehicle number, are shared with you before pickup.

Are all your cars air-conditioned? What is your AC policy?

All our cars are AC and sanitized before trips. The AC is available for your comfort during the ride, and you can ask the driver to adjust it as you like. Please tell us if anyone in your group has special comfort needs, so we can plan the right vehicle.

Are there night charges?

A night allowance may apply for travel between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM. Whether it applies to your trip, and how much, is confirmed when we give you the fare.

Is someone reachable during my trip?

Yes. Our team is available 24/7 by phone and WhatsApp. If you face any problem during the ride, contact us at any hour.

How many passengers and how much luggage can the car take?

The WagonR is a 4-seater, the Sedan is a 5-seater, Ertiga and Innova are 7-seaters, and Innova Crysta is an 8-seater. Tempo Travellers are available for bigger groups. Luggage space varies by car. Tell us your passenger count and luggage when booking so we assign the right vehicle.

Can I travel with children or elderly family members?

Of course. Our drivers are used to family and pilgrimage trips. Tell us in advance about anyone needing extra care or breaks, and we will plan the trip accordingly.

What if the cab breaks down or the driver is late?

We arrange an alternate cab of the same category or better, or give you a full refund if we cannot. Read the full details in our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

Cancellations & Payments

Refunds, payment modes and advance booking.
What payment modes do you accept?

We accept cash and UPI / bank transfer, as confirmed at booking. Please pay only to the account or UPI ID shared by Chamunda Cab on our official numbers.

Do I need to pay an advance to book?

For some bookings, especially outstation, multi-day and peak-season trips, we may ask for an advance to confirm the booking. The amount is told to you at the time of booking, and the balance is paid at the end of the trip or as agreed.

How much refund do I get if I cancel?
  • More than 24 hours before pickup: 100% refund
  • 12–24 hours before pickup: 50% refund
  • Within 12 hours of pickup, or a no-show: no refund

See the full Refund & Cancellation Policy.

How long does a refund take?

Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to the original payment mode.

Can I get a GST invoice?

Yes. Ask for a GST invoice when you book, and share your company name and GST number. It is available for corporate customers.

Will you ever ask for my OTP, card PIN or banking password?

No, never. Chamunda Cab does not ask for card PINs, OTPs or banking passwords. We do not store your card details. If anyone asks you for these in our name, do not share them and contact us on our official numbers.

CHAMUNDA CAB - SEO UPDATE
=========================
1. Upload everything in this folder to your web host (keep the routes/ folder).
2. Open tools/build.py, change DOMAIN = "https://www.chamundacab.in" to YOUR real domain,
   then run:  cd tools && python3 build.py   (output goes to tools/out - upload that instead).
   (Or find/replace the domain in sitemap.xml, robots.txt and the canonical/og tags.)
3. Add a business street address in the JSON-LD (index.html) if you have a shop/office.
4. Create images/ (hero.jpg, dzire.jpg, ertiga.jpg ...) - the original zip had none.
5. Submit sitemap.xml in Google Search Console, and create/verify a Google Business Profile
   (Rajkot) with the same phone numbers: Booking 8128295920, WhatsApp 6352179909.
Edit routes/distances/pricing in tools/data.py and per-km rates in fleet-data.js, then rebuild.

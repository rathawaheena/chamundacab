Privacy Policy

At Chamunda Cab Tourism, Rajkot, we respect your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you book a ride, how we use it, and how we keep it safe.

1.Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the Chamunda Cab Tourism website and to bookings made with us by phone, WhatsApp or email. Chamunda Cab Tourism (“Chamunda Cab”, “we”, “us”) is based in Rajkot, Gujarat, India.

By using our website or booking a ride, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use our services. Please also read our Terms & Conditions.

2.Information Collection

We collect only what we need to arrange and complete your ride.

  • Contact details. Your name, phone number, WhatsApp number and, if you share it, your email address.
  • Trip details. Pickup and drop locations, travel date and time, number of passengers, luggage, preferred vehicle and any special requests.
  • Flight or train details. If you book an airport or station transfer and share them, so that we can track arrival and avoid delays.
  • Messages and calls. The content of your WhatsApp messages, emails and calls with us about a booking or a query.
  • Payment information. A record that a payment was made, such as the amount, date and mode (cash or UPI/bank transfer). We do not collect or store your card numbers, PINs or banking passwords.
  • Billing details. Company name and GST number, if you ask for a GST invoice.
  • Technical information. When you visit our website, basic data such as your browser type, device and pages visited may be recorded by your browser or hosting provider.
  • Location. We use the pickup and drop addresses you give us. We do not track your location on our website unless you choose to share it.
You decide what to share with us. Some details, such as a contact number and the route, are needed to make a booking.

3.Use of Data

We use your information to:

  • Confirm, manage and complete your booking, including sending you the fare, driver and vehicle details.
  • Contact you about your trip by call, WhatsApp, SMS or email, for example about pickup time, delays, changes or cancellations.
  • Share the necessary trip details (such as your name, pickup point and contact number) with the driver assigned to your ride.
  • Prepare invoices, receipts and records of payments.
  • Provide customer support and respond to your questions, feedback or complaints.
  • Improve our website, routes and services, and keep our records accurate.
  • Prevent fraud and misuse, keep our drivers and passengers safe, and meet legal and tax obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Drivers and partners who carry out your trip, only for the details needed for that trip.
  • Service providers such as payment, messaging, website hosting or accounting services that help us run the business, and who are expected to keep your data confidential.
  • Authorities when required by law, a court order or a valid legal request, or to protect the rights and safety of our customers, drivers or Chamunda Cab.

We send promotional messages only if you have agreed to receive them, and you can opt out at any time.

4.Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Make the website work properly, for example remembering your preferences and form entries during a visit.
  • Understand in general how visitors use our website, so that we can improve it.
  • Load content and features from third parties, such as fonts, icons, maps and WhatsApp chat links.

You can control or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how parts of the website work. Third-party services that we link to or load content from, such as Google Maps or WhatsApp, have their own privacy and cookie policies, which we do not control.

5.Data Protection

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

  • Limited access. Only our team members and drivers who need your details to serve you can see them.
  • Careful sharing. We give drivers and service providers only the information needed for their task.
  • Retention. We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as the law requires for accounting and tax records, and then delete or anonymise it.
  • Children. Our services are meant to be booked by adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without a parent or guardian.
  • Fraud awareness. Chamunda Cab will never ask for your card PIN, OTP or banking password. Please confirm payment details only on our official numbers below.
No method of transmission over the internet or of electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please protect your own devices and accounts as well.

6.Your Choices & Rights

You can contact us at any time to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct information that is wrong or out of date.
  • Ask us to delete your information, unless we need to keep it for legal, tax or safety reasons.
  • Withdraw your consent for promotional messages or other optional uses of your data.
  • Make a complaint about how your information is handled.

We handle personal information in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We will respond to your request within a reasonable time.

Changes to this policy. We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page, with its “last updated” date.

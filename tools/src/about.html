Our story

A ride is more than a drive. It is trust on wheels.

In Gujarat a journey is rarely just a journey. It is a family heading to Dwarka for darshan, a business owner catching an early flight from Hirasar, a parent bringing a child home from Ahmedabad. When people step into a cab they hand us something precious: their time, their plans and their peace of mind.

Chamunda Cab Tourism, Rajkot, is built around the promise printed on our logo: Your Journey, Our Responsibility. That means a clean AC cabin, a driver who knows the highways, a fare confirmed before the wheels turn, and a real person on the phone at any hour.

Today we serve one-way, round-trip, local and airport rides on 220+ routes from Rajkot and Ahmedabad, and across Saurashtra, Kutch and South Gujarat.

How do I book a cab?

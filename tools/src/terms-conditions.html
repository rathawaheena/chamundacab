  • Refunds depend on the time left before pickup: more than 24 hours = 100% refund; 12–24 hours = 50% refund; within 12 hours or no-show = no refund. Refunds are made to the original payment mode within 5–7 business days. Full details are in our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

5.Passenger Responsibilities

  • Accurate information. Provide correct contact details, pickup point and passenger count, and be reachable by phone around pickup time.
  • Punctuality. Be ready at the agreed place and time. Late arrival may lead to waiting charges or cancellation as described above.
  • Care of the vehicle. Treat the vehicle with care. Smoking, alcohol, consumption of food that may spill, and carrying illegal, hazardous or inflammable goods are not allowed. You may be charged for cleaning or for damage caused by you or your group.
  • Respect for the driver. Behave courteously with the driver. We may end a trip if the driver’s or other passengers’ safety is at risk, and the fare up to that point remains payable.
  • Safe travel. Wear seat belts and follow the driver’s safety instructions. Our drivers follow traffic laws and speed limits, and will not be asked to break them.
  • Children and elders. Children must be accompanied by an adult. Please let us know in advance if anyone needs special assistance.
  • Valid ID. Carry a valid photo ID, and any permits or documents needed for the places you are travelling to.
  • Your belongings. Check the vehicle before leaving it. If you leave something behind, contact us promptly and we will help to recover it.
